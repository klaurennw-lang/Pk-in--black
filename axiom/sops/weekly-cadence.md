## Standup format (Monday 9am, 15 min)

Per active client (60 seconds each):
- What shipped last week vs. plan
- Anything red (missed delivery, tech failure, client friction)
- Anything to escalate

Then:
- Cross-client themes (what we're learning)
- Tooling/process issues to address this week

Hard stop at 15 min. If it runs over, the format is wrong.

---

## Friday review call (15 min, per client, Studio+)

Format:
1. Show this week's deliverables in a single Notion view
2. Client gives thumbs up / asks for revisions
3. Confirm next week's calendar at high level
4. Surface any business-side context that should change the plan

If client wants longer discussion, schedule a separate strategy call. Don't let this bloat — its job is approval, not strategy.

---

## When things slip

- **Strategist late:** delays everything downstream. Founder is on-call for Sunday calendar generation; never miss a Monday client publish.
- **Scripts late:** push back the production schedule, not the publish schedule (cut piece count, don't ship rushed work).
- **Cuts late:** Thursday EOD slips to Friday morning. Friday review still happens. Posting may slip a day; document it.
- **Client misses Friday review:** auto-approve after 4 hours past scheduled time, with a note. Otherwise we get blocked indefinitely.

## Why a fixed cadence beats "ad hoc when ready"

- Predictability is the product. Clients pay for "I know what I'm getting Friday."
- It lets us scale: every new client slots into the same week. No bespoke schedules.
- It surfaces problems fast. Missed Tuesday script delivery = visible immediately.
- It compounds: every Sunday's analyst report informs the next Sunday's calendar. The loop only works if the days are fixed.

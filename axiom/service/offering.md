# The Offering

Three tiers, monthly retainer, clear delivery contract per tier. Onboarding is always a separate paid sprint that precedes the retainer (see `sops/onboarding-sprint.md`).

---

## Tier 1 — Foundation

**Who it's for:** Solo creators or small brands who already produce content and need a smarter, faster brain — not a full pipeline.

**Active agents:** Strategist, Researcher, Scriptwriter

**What the client receives every week:**
- Updated content calendar (next 14 days, all platforms they're on)
- Hook bank: 25 fresh hooks, scored against their voice
- 5 ready-to-shoot scripts (long-form OR short-form, their choice of mix)
- 1 trend brief: what's moving in their niche this week + recommended angles
- Monthly: brand voice doc maintenance + competitor scan refresh

**What the client does:**
- Records / posts / edits themselves (or with their existing team)
- Approves scripts before they're "shipped" to their drafts folder
- 30-min monthly strategy call

**Delivery contract:**
- Calendar delivered every Monday by 12pm ET
- Scripts delivered Tuesday & Thursday
- Hook bank refreshed every Friday
- Trend brief delivered Sunday night

**Headcount on Axiom side:** 1 strategist (part-time), agent stack does the heavy lifting

---

## Tier 2 — Studio

**Who it's for:** Established creators or brands who want raw input → finished output, fully handled. They send footage and a Loom; they get back posted content.

**Active agents:** Strategist, Researcher, Scriptwriter, Producer, Editor, Distributor

**What the client receives every week:**
- Everything in Foundation, plus:
- Full edit + caption + thumbnail for every long-form piece (up to 2/week)
- Short-form derivatives (up to 10/week) — Reels, Shorts, TikToks, X clips
- AI b-roll generated to match script needs
- Music + SFX bed
- Multi-platform format conversion (vertical, square, horizontal)
- Scheduled posting across their connected platforms
- Weekly A/B test on thumbnails OR hooks (whichever has lower data confidence)
- Monthly performance report (rolled into Empire if upgraded)

**What the client does:**
- Records the talking-head footage (or sends raw clips)
- Approves the weekly batch in a 15-min review on Friday
- 60-min monthly strategy call

**Delivery contract:**
- Cuts ready for review by Thursday EOD
- Posts go live on the scheduled cadence (defined per client)
- Brand voice + asset library maintained continuously
- Maximum 24-hour turnaround on revisions to any single piece

**Headcount on Axiom side:** 1 strategist + 1 editor-supervisor (the human who approves agent output before it ships)

---

## Tier 3 — Empire

**Who it's for:** Creators running a business behind the channel — courses, products, services. They need content as growth infrastructure, not just posts.

**Active agents:** All 8 (Strategist, Researcher, Scriptwriter, Producer, Editor, Distributor, Community, Analyst)

**What the client receives every week:**
- Everything in Studio, plus:
- DM triage + first-touch responses (in client voice, within brand-safe templates)
- Comment classification + flagging (leads, fan, troll, complaint, opportunity)
- FAQ auto-replies for common questions
- Lead capture pushed into client CRM
- Weekly analyst report: what worked, what didn't, why, what to make next
- Custom dashboard (live metrics across all platforms)
- Monthly executive review with founder

**Optional add-ons (priced separately):**
- **Brand-deal pipeline** — outreach, negotiation prep, contract review
- **Course launch sprint** — content engine specifically for a launch window
- **Paid-ad creative engine** — ad variants, hook tests, creative refresh
- **Sponsor outreach** — researched lists, customized pitches

**What the client does:**
- Records footage, approves weekly batch, joins monthly exec review
- Reviews flagged DMs/comments daily (5-min queue, optional)

**Delivery contract:**
- All Studio guarantees, plus:
- DM/comment queue cleared daily by 9am ET
- Weekly analyst report delivered Sunday night
- Dashboard live 24/7

**Headcount on Axiom side:** 1 strategist + 1 editor-supervisor + 1 community manager (shared across multiple Empire clients)

---

## Why these three (not five, not one)

- **Foundation** is the entry point. Low-risk for the client, high margin for Axiom, easy to upsell.
- **Studio** is the flagship. Where most clients land and stay. The pipeline is the product.
- **Empire** is the growth ladder. Few clients, big retainer, deep relationship. This is where Axiom becomes irreplaceable infrastructure.

There is intentionally no "à la carte" tier. Custom work kills margins and fragments delivery. Clients pick a tier or they're not a fit.
